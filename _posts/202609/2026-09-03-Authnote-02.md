Hi there, Neithium here.

As promised, the hiatus is over. Taking a step back gave me exactly the perspective I needed to rethink how I want to run the Neithium Archives. 

Moving forward, I am dropping out the weekly publishing goal. Forcing a post out every seven days was actively hurting the quality of the content and making the blogging process a bit too mechanical and soul-less. From now on, I will publish when I have something genuinely valuable to share. 

Expect much niche and richer content and more insights about the projects and research I'm currently working on rather than filler content meant to meet a deadline. I am aiming for atleast a single quality post every month, but again, it depends on many factors.

Thanks for sticking around during the downtime, will be dropping the next post soon.

Take care and happy hacking !!
