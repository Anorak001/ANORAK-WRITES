It was evening of 30th july 2026, I had watched the documentary *The Social Dilemma* on Netflix and was left with curious thought: "Is it really that damaging?". I decided that I should hop on to Instagram since I had never used the platform before and see for myself what the hype is all about. I intially aimed of using it for 30 days and if it was not useful, I would delete the account. The platform was so engaging that I ended up using it for 46 days before finally deleting the account.

The app was never consumer focused, it was designed to maximize engagement and attention. Though there are other advantages such as actively connecting with your long lost peers, friends and distant relatives, the cons outweight the pros by a significant margin. Below are some of my key observations during this phase.

---

### Key Observations:

#### 1. The 15–20 Reel Pattern Shift
After you watch the first 15 to 25 reels,the algorithm breaks the user's curated feed and introduces test vectors which are nothing but randomized content varying at extreme wavelengths to test if new engagement hooks can be established and if they can be suggested in the feed later on.

#### 2. The Audio-Baiting
A majority of short form videos usually lacks content. It relies entirely on external stimulation triggers, a typical high-tempo Phonk audio or trending audio blocks to mask the absence of real value. Stripped of the audio track, the content collapses.

### 3. The Withdrawal Spike
Lets say you stopping engaging with the platform for a few days due to some important work. The platform is built in such an engaging way that when you log back on (for me it was day 4), you would recieve a highly curated feed of content that would make you hit your peak screen-time on that app.

#### 4. Zero-Retention  
Try recalling the last 5th video you had watched on the platform. You can't, because the content is designed to be consumed and forgotten. Only the engagement is maximized, not the retention factor which results in your time being wasted without any value addition.

---

### The Performative Illusion & Parasocial Load

The Social platform incentivizes artificial signaling over real-world competence.

* **The Curated Reality:** Users project an optimized, performative version of their lives that bears zero resemblance to their offline reality. What you see about a person on the platform is mostly not who they usually tend to be.

* **Depletion of Ambition:** Watching someone else climb a mountain while sitting in a bedroom does not inspire action, it prematurely satisfies the brain's reward mechanism. Consuming visual output kills the internal fuel required to actually execute the real-world task.

* **Targeted Emotion Manipulation:** The algorithm actively detects isolation or fatigue, immediately curating the feed with targeted, emotionally manipulative content (loneliness reels, relationship drama) to exploit vulnerability and hold attention.

---

### Data Privacy: 

Maintaining an active Instagram profile creates unnecessary surface area for social engineering and behavioral tracking.

- Publicly displaying a face, follower graph, following networks, and location tags allows third parties to easily map personal habits, relationships, and routines. Sometimes, even your followers can be used to map your entire social network and predict the relationships between them.

* also, there is no benefit a for person with no actual curated profile to be on instagram. Creating a blank profile mostly results in wasting time and attention, which could be used for other productive work.
 
 
#### Few metrics that I actually tracked during this period:

* **Peak Screen Time (Single Day):** 4 hours, 31 minutes.
* **Daily Average Consumption:** ~1 hour, 15 minutes.

** the above data is taken from android's digital wellbeing app which tracks the screen time of each app.

---

### The 30-Day Retention Trap

Upon requesting permanent account deletion, Meta enforces a 30-day grace period before wiping data from servers. This is not a security feature, it is a retention mechanic designed to capitalize on withdrawal loops. Logging in once during this window automatically cancels the deletion sequence.

Treating the account as dead and refusing to initiate a login patch completely neutralizes it.

---

### My thoughts:

To have a faith that you would overcome the addiction of a social media platform means that you'd be doubting the skilled engineer at a company whose whole compensation is based on maximizing your retention period. I'd say that staying on the platform as a consumer has a lot of cons than the advantages.
